> **BRAND VOICE RULES — Read before touching any copy**
>
> ✅ First person singular. "I build" not "we build." This is Prince Kofi's personal brand.
> ✅ Short declarative sentences. "Design That Thinks." not "We create thoughtful and strategic designs."
> ✅ Confident. Never hedging. Never "we try to" or "we aim to."
> ✅ Precision over decoration. Every word earns its place.
> ✅ African excellence, global ambition. Neither apologetic nor performative.
> ✅ Scientific vocabulary meets creative language. "Rigour." "Structured." "Purposeful."
> ❌ Never: "passionate", "amazing", "incredible", "we believe in", "we're dedicated to"
> ❌ Never: "psyche evo" or "PsycheEvo" — always "psycheevo." (lowercase, with period)
> ❌ Never write the period without the wordmark: it is "psycheevo." not "psycheevo"
